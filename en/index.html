Our Story

From Palermo to the world

Kajue was born in the heart of Palermo Soho with a simple idea: serve specialty coffee without pretensions. We believe a great espresso doesn't need long explanations — just excellent beans and someone who knows how to bring out the best in them.

Our team travels to origin, seeking lots that tell stories. From Ethiopia to Colombia, Brazil to Guatemala — every cup has a journey behind it. Here, you enjoy it calmly, no rush, as it should be.

Over 3,000 reviews tell us we're doing something right. But what matters most to us is that you come back. Always.
